Questions

Frequently asked

What do I need to get started?

Just what you already know: full names, rough dates and places for parents and grandparents, plus any old photos, obituaries, family Bibles or military papers. Even a little helps.

Can you guarantee how far back you'll get?

No honest genealogist can. Some families are well documented for centuries; others hit a wall where records were lost or never kept. You buy a set number of research hours, and we'll tell you clearly what we found and what's left to search.

What are "record fees"?

Some records cost money to order, like certificates, courthouse copies or archive fees. These are billed to you at cost with no markup, and we ask before spending more than a small set amount.

How long does it take?

Reports usually arrive within 3–6 weeks. Heirloom Books take a little longer: after research and writing, you approve a digital proof, then printing and shipping take about 2–3 weeks.

Do you work with families outside Maryland?

Yes. Most records today are searched online, so we can research families from all over the United States.

Is my family's information kept private?

Always. Information about living people is never shared or published, and we never show your report or book to anyone without your written permission.
